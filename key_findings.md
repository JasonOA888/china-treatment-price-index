# Key findings: ChinaHealth Open China Treatment Price Index 2026 (v1)

All USD figures at 1 USD = 6.6681 CNY (2026-10-26), rounded per METHODOLOGY. Record IDs refer to `china_price_index_2026_v1.csv`. Patient-reported figures are not used here.

1. **A public all-inclusive laparoscopic gallbladder removal in Shenzhen costs CNY 14,100 (about $2,100), below even the US Medicare national average of $3,661 to $6,807, while Shanghai private hospitals list CNY 45,000 to 90,000 (about $6,700 to $13,500).**
   Sources: HKU-Shenzhen Hospital package list 2025-06-04 (cpi26-075); Jiahui fee schedule 2024-01-15 (cpi26-077); Shanghai United Family bundle (cpi26-078); CMS Procedure Price Lookup, CPT 47562.
   *Caveat:* Medicare is a payment floor, not a cash price; the United Family bundle image dates from a 2019 upload and may be stale.

2. **The same MRI can cost roughly 4 to 31 times more in a private hospital than the official public level: about CNY 500 (about $70) in public hospitals versus CNY 2,000 to 15,525 (about $300 to $2,300) at United Family and Jiahui.**
   Sources: NHSA 2026-09-18 (cpi26-045); United Family Beijing, Beijing DCU and Shenzhen price lists, Jiahui fee schedule (cpi26-049 to cpi26-052).
   *Caveat:* The NHSA figure is a plain scan "about 500 yuan" level; private prices cover one body part and some include contrast or film, so scopes are not identical.

3. **Coronary stent procurement cut the average total hospital bill for PCI from close to CNY 60,000 to CNY 44,000 (about $6,600) in eight high-volume hospitals.**
   Source: NHSA, real-world study led by Fuwai Hospital, 2026-05-19 (cpi26-001).
   *Caveat:* Compares 2019 and 2022 cohorts at top centres; costs for foreign self-pay patients or smaller hospitals may differ, and no citable US PCI reference was found.

4. **Childbirth in Chinese public hospitals averaged CNY 3,547 for a vaginal delivery and CNY 9,119 for a C-section (about $530 and $1,400), against US employer-plan averages of $15,712 and $28,998; private United Family Shenzhen packages are CNY 66,000 and 88,000 (about $9,900 and $13,200).**
   Sources: NHSA 2024-04-13 (2021 data, cpi26-065, cpi26-069); Peterson-KFF 2025-09-09; Shenzhen United Family price list (cpi26-068, cpi26-074).
   *Caveat:* KFF totals include prenatal and postpartum care, and the NHSA averages are 2021 figures for local patients, so the gap is overstated for a like-for-like delivery package.

5. **CAR-T list prices in China are CNY 999,000 to 1.29 million per infusion (about $149,800 to $193,500), roughly 40% of US list prices of $373,000 to $475,000.**
   Sources: Int J Health Policy Manag 2024, PMC11365067 (cpi26-015); EBMT/EHA CAR-T Cell Handbook, NBK584170.
   *Caveat:* Drug price only, different products in each country, and hospital care is extra; foreign patients have reported different all-in prices (cpi26-017, low confidence).
