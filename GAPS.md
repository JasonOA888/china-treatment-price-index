# Known gaps, v1.0.0 (for v1.1 / v2 research)

## Missing tiers
- Only tier a: PCI, CABG, TAVR, SVT ablation, PELD spine, proton therapy, VATS lobectomy, gastrectomy, rectal cancer surgery, breast cancer surgery, thyroid cancer surgery, chemotherapy cycle, dental implant, implant crown, IVF, PET/CT, colon polypectomy, pediatric liver transplant.
- No tier b (public VIP/international) for most surgery; only cataract, checkups, MRI, CT, endoscopy and delivery have one.
- No private-hospital price for dental implants, IVF, cardiac or cancer procedures.
- Only tier c: ACL reconstruction, LASIK, ICL.

## Missing procedures / weak coverage
- All-on-4 / full-arch implants: no citable price (governance documents exclude full-arch from the CNY 4,500 cap).
- Dental crown (non-implant) and root canal: not collected.
- TCM course: only per-session acupuncture ceilings (Liaoning) and one private price (Jiahui).
- CABG: only an old single-centre study (2014-2016, USD) and a surgeon's range; no current official total.
- TAVR: valve list price only, no all-in hospital total after 2026 price cuts.
- Chemotherapy: only Liaoning day-ward reference costs for breast cancer regimens.

## US references missing (27 procedures)
PCI, TAVR, SVT ablation, spine, ACL, arthroscopy, proton, lobectomy, gastrectomy, rectal and breast cancer surgery, thyroidectomy, chemo cycle, dental implant and crown, LASIK, SMILE, ICL, checkup, CT (Medicare CPT 74177 was requested but not captured), appendectomy, hemorrhoidectomy, hysterectomy, polypectomy, acupuncture, liver transplant.

- Shanghai United Family surgery bundles: images uploaded 2019-06 (inferred from file path), still linked live.
- Liaoning day-surgery reference costs: 2022. Fujian VIP birth package: 2019. West China LDRP package: 2018.
- Undated pages: Tongji SMILE (price set 2018-07-17), Xiamen Eye Center, PUMCH, SRRSH, United Family Shenzhen and DCU.
- Jiahui maternity package page (jiahui.com/en/package/31) returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-27, so earlier Jiahui delivery prices (CNY 45,000 / 70,000) were not included.
